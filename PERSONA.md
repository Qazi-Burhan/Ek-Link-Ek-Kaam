# Parent learner persona (hypothesis)

> This is a hypothetical design persona, not a real interview participant. No interviews or user studies have been conducted or are claimed here.

## Context

**Name:** Amina (fictional)  
**Situation:** A parent who uses a smartphone for calls and messaging but may need guidance when using an unfamiliar app or completing a multi-step task.

## Potential needs and challenges

- Small text and crowded screens may be difficult to follow.
- Several instructions presented at once can be overwhelming.
- Unfamiliar controls may make a learner worry about making a mistake.
- Repeatedly asking someone else for help may make it harder to practise independently.
- Urdu and English language preferences vary; the learner should be able to switch without losing progress.

## Design hypotheses

- Presenting one clear step at a time may reduce cognitive load.
- Large text, touch targets, and a high-contrast interface may support easier use.
- Urdu/English switching and optional speech may make instructions more accessible.
- Gradually reducing help per step may support practice while keeping hints and answers available.
- Local-only learning progress may give families greater privacy and control.

These are design hypotheses to validate with parents. They must not be presented as research findings until actual interviews or usability sessions have been conducted and documented.

## Suggested interview questions

1. Which phone or app tasks are difficult to do without help?
2. What makes instructions easy or difficult to follow on a phone?
3. Would you prefer Urdu, English, or to switch between both?
4. When would you want a hint, a full answer, or a person to contact?
5. Does saving learning progress only on your own device feel clear and comfortable?
6. After practising a task, what would make you feel ready to try it without guidance?

## Decisions to validate

- One instruction per screen
- Readable Urdu typography and a clear English font
- A persistent language switch
- Optional voice playback with visible text alternatives
- Independent assistance levels for each guide step
- Explicit consent before opening WhatsApp
- Clear notice that guide text is visible to anyone who has the share link
